Non-executive representation

Good boards need someone who will ask the awkward commercial question. We bring an independent view, grounded in years of running sales in the energy market.

Start a conversation

We sit outside the internal politics and the decisions already made, which means we can test the thinking honestly. Our focus is where growth is credible, where risk is building and whether the commercial engine can deliver the plan.

An objective voice

We are not tied to legacy decisions or historic assumptions. That independence lets us challenge constructively and ask the questions that often go unasked, from a sales and market point of view rather than a purely financial one.

Practical, not theoretical

We review strategy, test market positioning, assess investment cases and sense-check the assumptions behind big decisions. We also look at whether commercial discipline runs through the business, from pipeline and pricing through to delivery.

Insight and access

Our background across UK energy and wider B2B markets means we can also connect boards with specialist partners, potential customers, sector experts and investors.

Common questions

Quick answers

What is a non-executive director?

A non-executive director sits on the board without a day-to-day management role. They bring independent challenge, experience and contacts, and hold the executive team to account.

When does a business need one?

The usual signs: the board lacks commercial or sales experience; you are preparing for investment or sale; growth plans need an outside challenge; investors want an independent voice on the board.

What do you get at the end?

Typically: regular board participation and challenge; review of strategy and commercial plans; support to the CEO and commercial lead; introductions where they help; a clear view on commercial risk. See how pricing works.
