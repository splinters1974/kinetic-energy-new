Busy, but not growing the way you should be

The market for energy and decarbonisation has never been bigger. Yet plenty of good businesses with good products are stuck. In our experience it is rarely the market. It is focus.

Too many markets, not enough focus

Every sector looks like an opportunity, so the team chases all of them. Resources get spread thin and nobody wins anywhere convincingly.

A great product nobody can explain

The technology is strong, but the story is about kit and kilowatts. Buyers, and especially finance directors, cannot see why they should pick you.

A full pipeline that never closes

Pipeline value looks healthy and the forecast moves every month. Weak deals linger because nobody wants to let them go. Our view: lose it fast.

How we work

Clarity first. Then the discipline to deliver it.

No long reports that sit in a drawer. We work alongside your leadership team and stay until the change is real.

Get clear

An honest look at where you win, where you lose and why. We talk to your team, your numbers and, where we can, your customers.

Choose fewer things

Agree the markets, proposition and deals worth your effort, and what to stop. One plan that sales, marketing and delivery all recognise.

Make it stick

Process, pricing, coaching and hands-on support on live deals, so the plan shows up in the pipeline and not just the board pack.

What people ask us first

What does Kinetic Energy Consulting do?

We are a commercial and sales consultancy for energy businesses. We help owners, MDs and boards decide where to focus, sharpen what they offer and build the sales discipline to win. See our services.

Who do you work with?

Mostly UK energy and decarbonisation businesses: solution providers, energy consultancies, engineering and FM firms, new market entrants, investors and leadership teams going through change. See who we work with.

How are you different from a marketing agency?

We do not sell rebrands or monthly content. We work on the commercial side: which markets to target, how deals are priced and structured, and how the sales team qualifies and wins. Marketing follows from that.

What is the “clarity problem”?

Most energy businesses are not short of market demand. They are chasing too many markets, describing themselves in technical terms and carrying deals that will never close. Growth usually comes from doing fewer things properly.

How do we start?

With a conversation. If we can help, you get a short written proposal with a clear scope and price. See how pricing works or get in touch.

Got a commercial problem that needs a clear answer?

Start with a conversation. No pitch, no obligation. Just an honest view on where the growth is and what is getting in the way.
