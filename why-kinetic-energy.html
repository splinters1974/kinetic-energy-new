Why Kinetic Energy?

Plenty of consultants can point out problems. We have sat in the sales director’s chair, so we help you fix them.

Start a conversation
What we bring

Experience from the inside

Thirty years in sales and fifteen in energy means we understand the pressure of hitting targets, closing deals and handing over to delivery. Every piece of advice is shaped by that reality.

Commercial clarity

We cut through complexity to find the numbers and models that drive profit. Pricing, PPA and funding structures, margin and go-to-market design, made simple enough to act on.

Sales discipline

Great strategy means nothing without great execution. Stronger pipelines, higher conversion and teams that know exactly where to focus, built on proven methods such as Miller Heiman, SPIN and Challenger.

A story that lands

We help you say what you do in a way the right customers understand and trust, from the first meeting to the final proposal.

What is in it for you

Clarity, confidence and consistency

  • See opportunities sooner and make decisions faster, with evidence rather than opinion.
  • Stronger commercial foundations, from pricing and deal structures to propositions and positioning.
  • Teams that sell with confidence, convert more and build lasting customer relationships.
  • Time back to run the business, with an experienced hand shaping growth and challenging assumptions.
Straight talking

What we are not

Not a marketing agency

We will help you decide what to say and to whom. We will not sell you a rebrand or a monthly content package.

Not a generic growth consultancy

No borrowed frameworks from other industries. We work in energy, with people who know how energy is bought.

Not theory

We judge our work by what changes in your pipeline, your margins and your team, not by the thickness of the report.
