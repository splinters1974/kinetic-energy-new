Where we help

Four areas we usually start with

Management teams in energy businesses are often very good at the technical story and less convincing on the commercial one. We give investors an independent view of how the business really wins work, how reliable the pipeline is and what would drive growth.

01

Assess commercial readiness

We review propositions, pricing and sales structures to find where growth is being held back. That gives a clear view of commercial maturity and the levers that will move the numbers.

02

Strengthen sales discipline

Pipeline management, sales governance and reporting that give investors reliable visibility of performance and forecasts they can trust.

03

Accelerate growth

Practical go-to-market improvement plans covering proposition, team structure and target focus, built to deliver measurable revenue uplift.

04

Create lasting value

Commercial capability that lasts beyond a funding round. Sales frameworks and leadership behaviours that protect and grow value ahead of exit.
