Sales process and pipeline

A full pipeline is not the same as a healthy one. We help you build a sales process that tells you the truth, early.

Start a conversation

Most pipelines are full of deals that will never close. Our view is simple: lose it fast. Qualify hard, drop the weak opportunities early and spend your best people on the deals you can actually win.

Look at how sales really works today

We start with what happens now, not what the process document says. Where deals come from, how they are qualified, where they stall, how handovers work and how confident people really are in the numbers they report.

Most businesses already have the right ingredients. They are just used differently by every salesperson, which is where the forecast stops meaning anything.

Build a process people will use

We design or tighten a process that fits your market and the complexity of what you sell. Clear stage definitions, the evidence needed to move a deal forward, who owns what and the right amount of governance. Enough structure to give you control, not so much admin that the team works around it.

Where it helps, we set this up in your CRM so the process and the reporting are the same thing.

Make the forecast hold

The end result is fewer surprises at month end, better conversion and a pipeline the board can trust. Sales leaders get clarity, salespeople get focus and the business gets a more predictable route to growth.

Common questions

Quick answers

What is sales process and pipeline optimisation?

A sales process is the set of stages an opportunity moves through from first contact to signed contract, with clear rules for what has to be true before it moves on. Pipeline optimisation means making those stages honest, so the forecast can be trusted.

When does a business need it?

The usual signs: the forecast moves every month and nobody is surprised; deals sit at the same stage for quarters; pipeline value looks great but revenue does not follow; each salesperson qualifies differently.

What do you get at the end?

Typically: sales stage definitions with exit criteria; a qualification framework your team will use; pipeline review and forecast rhythm; CRM changes and reporting that match the process; coaching for sales managers to run it. See how pricing works.
