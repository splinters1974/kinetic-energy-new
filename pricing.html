Common questions

Before you get in touch

Who actually does the work?

Senior people who have run sales and commercial teams themselves. You are not handed to a junior team after the first meeting.

What if we are not sure what we need?

That is normal. Most clients come with a symptom, such as a stalled pipeline or slipping margins, not a brief. A first conversation and a short review usually show where to start.

Are we tied into a long contract?

No. Most clients start with a short, fixed piece of work. If it helps, we keep going. If it does not, nobody is locked in.

Do you only work with energy businesses?

Energy is where we know most. We also work with businesses in other complex B2B markets where the sale looks similar. See who we work with.

How do we know it will pay for itself?

We agree what success looks like before we start, in terms you can measure. See where the return comes from.

Want a price for your project?

Tell us what you are trying to fix. We will come back with an honest view of the scope and cost.
