Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information. Last updated November 2026.

1. Who we are

We are Kinetic Energy Consulting Ltd, registered in England and Wales. For data protection purposes we are the data controller of your personal information. If you have any questions about this policy, email enquiries@kinetic-energy.co.uk.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number and company when you fill in our contact form or get in touch.
  • Communication records: anything you tell us by email, phone or social media.
  • Client information: if you become a client, the details we need to deliver our services and manage the relationship.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry.
  • To deliver our services and manage contracts with clients.
  • To send you updates, but only if you have asked us to.
  • To meet legal and regulatory requirements.

We only use your data where we have a lawful basis: usually because it is needed for a contract, you have given consent, or we have a legitimate business interest.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell or trade your personal data. We only share it with:

  • Suppliers who help run our website, forms and email. Our contact form is processed by Formspree, and the website is hosted by Netlify.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers.
  • Regulators or law enforcement where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it. For clients that is usually the length of the relationship plus up to six years, to meet legal and accounting obligations. After that we delete or anonymise it securely.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not run analytics that identify you. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
