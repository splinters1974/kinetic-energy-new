Experience

Thirty years in sales leadership. Fifteen years in the UK energy market. Hands-on, not hands-off.

Founder and Managing Director

Martyn Sheridan

Martyn has spent thirty years in sales leadership and fifteen at the sharp end of the UK energy market. He has seen the sector move from traditional supply to decentralised energy, front and behind-the-meter infrastructure, and now digital and AI-driven services.

He has worked with national and international energy businesses, large industrial groups, public sector organisations, private equity and venture capital investors. His work ranges from go-to-market strategy and pricing to board-level negotiation on complex commercial deals, across solar, CHP, microgrids, battery storage and energy efficiency.

Along the way he has led businesses with more than £100m in annual revenue and grown pipelines from hundreds of megawatts to multi-gigawatt scale. He is trained in Miller Heiman, SPIN and Challenger selling.

People who have worked with him talk about how he listens, brings teams with him and keeps things simple. He would say that simplifying complex propositions is the job. If a customer cannot understand it, they will not buy it.
