Our story

Kinetic Energy Consulting was built on one observation from years inside energy businesses: ambition is rarely the problem. Focus is.

The people behind Kinetic Energy Consulting have spent their careers leading sales, commercial and marketing teams, much of it in the UK energy market. Over and over, we stepped into businesses with strong technology, capable people and real ambition that were still not growing the way they should.

The cause was nearly always the same. Strategy, proposition and execution had drifted apart. The business was chasing too many markets, describing itself in technical terms customers did not care about, and carrying a pipeline full of deals that were never going to close.

The fix was rarely more activity. It was clarity. Deciding where to play, being able to say why a customer should choose you, and having the discipline to drop what does not fit.

Kinetic Energy Consulting exists to bring that clarity to energy businesses, and then to stay long enough to make it real. The sector does not need more theory. It needs delivery.

What we believe

Three ideas behind everything we do

01

Clarity before activity

More campaigns, more bids and more hires will not fix a business that is unclear about where it wins. Get that right first.

02

Fewer things, done properly

The fastest growth usually comes from stopping things. Fewer markets, fewer bids, fewer messages, each done well.

03

Lose it fast

Weak opportunities cost more than the deals you lose. Qualify hard, let go early and spend your best people where you can win.
