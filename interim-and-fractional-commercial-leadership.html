Interim and fractional leadership

Sometimes you need a senior commercial leader now, not in six months, and not necessarily full time. That is what this is for.

Start a conversation

We step in with a clear mandate to steady the team, set direction and put structure in place, then hand it back to a permanent team when the time is right. You get senior capability without the cost, delay or risk of a permanent hire.

Understand what you are facing

It might be rapid growth, a leadership gap, a restructure, investor pressure or a commercial function that has lost its way. We agree what needs to happen, by when, and how success will be judged.

Work inside the business

This is hands-on. We work directly with sales, marketing, finance and operations to fix friction points, tighten forecasting and build processes that will outlast our time with you. We challenge where needed and support where it helps.

Leave it stronger

For boards and investors, it means commercial performance is being led by someone who has done it before in complex, high-pressure markets. For the team, it brings clarity and pace. For the business, it buys time to hire or restructure on your own terms.

Common questions

Quick answers

What is interim and fractional commercial leadership?

An interim commercial leader runs your sales or commercial function full time for a fixed period. A fractional leader does the same job part time, for example one or two days a week.

When does a business need it?

The usual signs: your sales or commercial director has left; growth has outpaced the leadership team; investors want a stronger commercial grip; you need a senior hire but cannot justify full time yet.

What do you get at the end?

Typically: a 30, 60 and 90-day plan agreed with the board; hands-on leadership of the sales and commercial team; forecasting and governance that will last; recruitment support for the permanent role; a clean handover. See how pricing works.
