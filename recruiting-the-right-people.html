Recruiting the right people

A bad senior sales hire costs far more than the salary. We help you define the job properly and pick people who will actually raise the bar.

Start a conversation

Most hiring mistakes start before the first interview, with a vague brief. We help you be clear about what the role must deliver, then give you an experienced, independent view of who can deliver it.

Define the role properly

What does the job really need to achieve in the first year? Which behaviours matter most? Where does it sit in the strategy? We challenge the assumptions that sit behind a lot of job descriptions, so the brief is grounded in reality.

Support the process

We help shape the job description, sense-check shortlists and design interviews that test what matters. Our role is not to replace your judgement or your recruiter. It is to bring thirty years of hiring and leading sales teams to help you see past a polished CV.

Protect performance

Good hiring avoids the cost of mis-hires, reduces churn and builds a team that can grow without constant firefighting. Whether you are scaling, replacing a key role or rebuilding after change, the aim is people who strengthen the culture you want.

Common questions

Quick answers

What is sales recruitment support?

It is help with hiring sales and commercial people: defining what the role must deliver, shaping the job description, reviewing shortlists and designing interviews that test what matters.

When does a business need it?

The usual signs: you are hiring your first or next sales leader; recent hires have not worked out; the job description was copied from a competitor; you are not sure what good looks like for the role.

What do you get at the end?

Typically: role scorecard with first-year outcomes; job description and advert wording; shortlist review; structured interview questions and exercises; onboarding plan for the first 90 days. See how pricing works.
