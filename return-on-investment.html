Return on investment

Consultancy should pay for itself. Here are the six ways ours usually does, often within the first few months.

Where the return comes from

Six ways the work pays back

01

Better sales results, quickly

Tighter qualification, sharper messaging and a disciplined process mean your team closes more of the right deals and stops wasting time on dead ends. Faster cycles, cleaner pipelines and more predictable revenue.

02

Protected margin

Commercial models, pricing structures and value cases that stand up to scrutiny give your team the confidence to negotiate without giving margin away just to get the deal signed.

03

Clear market focus

Many businesses spread themselves too thin. Finding the markets where you can win, and dropping the rest, cuts wasted spend and internal confusion. Focus alone often pays for the work.

04

Less delivery risk

A strategy only has value if it is delivered. We fix handovers between sales, engineering, commercial and delivery, so fewer deals go wrong after they are won.

05

Senior capability without the overhead

A senior commercial hire can cost £70k to £150k in salary before recruitment fees and months of onboarding. You get senior experience from day one, without the long-term cost or the risk of a mis-hire.

06

Outside challenge

Internal teams provide detail. We provide the challenge and outside perspective that helps leaders make decisions they will actually stand behind. That alone often pays for itself several times over.
