Leadership teams in transition

Mergers, restructures and market pivots, where the commercial engine has stalled and the team needs to find its focus again.

Start a conversation
Where we help

Four areas we usually start with

Change exposes commercial weaknesses quickly. Ownership gets blurred, targets stop matching the strategy and good people leave. We help leadership teams see clearly what has broken and fix it in the right order.

01

Diagnose the problem

Find where commercial alignment has broken down. Unclear ownership, a weak pipeline, misaligned targets or a proposition that no longer fits the market.

02

Rebuild commercial clarity

Redefine customer focus, rebuild go-to-market structures and line up sales, marketing and delivery behind one clear plan.

03

Embed accountability

Performance frameworks, dashboards and governance that make ownership and progress visible, so decisions are based on evidence, not opinion.

04

Regain momentum

Coach the team through delivery, from live bids to internal communication, and rebuild confidence inside the business and with customers.
